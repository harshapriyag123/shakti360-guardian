# 90-Second Winning Pitch

## Hook
**Women's safety technology should not require choosing between safety, privacy and battery life.**

## Problem
Many safety apps begin at the SOS moment. They often rely on manual panic actions or persistent location sharing and rarely help before or after an incident.

## Solution
**Shakti360 Guardian** is a battery-aware, privacy-first safety platform for iOS, Android and Web covering:

**Prepare → Protect → Respond → Connect → Document → Recover**

## Technical differentiation
- event-driven journey sessions instead of permanent tracking
- deterministic escalation policy rather than autonomous AI emergency decisions
- Context Fusion, Cyber, Evidence, Pattern and Privacy agents
- SafeWord + Trusted Circle workflows
- hospital/police/pharmacy resource layer
- cross-platform Expo architecture
- offline/low-power architecture path
- measurable impact dashboard

## Closing line
**AI assists. She decides.**
