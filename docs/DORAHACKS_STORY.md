# Shakti360: DoraHacks story

## The problem

Many safety products begin at the moment of crisis and rely on permanent tracking. Women need support before, during, and after an uncertain situation without surrendering continuous location access.

## Our insight

Personal safety is not one red button. It is preparation, temporary coordination, explainable escalation, access to nearby help, private documentation, and recovery.

## What Shakti360 does

Shakti360 starts a time-bound safety journey, adapts sampling to battery and movement context, requests check-ins, and applies a deterministic escalation policy. The user stays in control of guardian sharing and emergency-service calls.

## Technical innovation

- Adaptive, non-continuous battery policy with explicit inputs and outputs
- Expiring Guardian Circle invitations and location sessions
- Deterministic, versioned escalation decisions
- Explainable rule-first scam screening
- Live OpenStreetMap support resources with transparent provenance
- Judge Mode that exposes otherwise invisible safety decisions

## Privacy and accessibility

Location sharing is session-based. Quick Exit replaces sensitive UI immediately. Critical actions have large targets, text labels, high-contrast emergency states, and do not rely on color alone.
