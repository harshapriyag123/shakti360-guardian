# Judge Q&A

**Why not continuously track GPS?**  
Battery, privacy and adoption. Shakti360 is session-scoped and event-driven.

**What happens if AI hallucinates?**  
AI does not control critical escalation. Escalation is deterministic and testable.

**Does it call police automatically?**  
No. It surfaces verified local options and follows user-controlled trusted-circle policies.

**Are you predicting dangerous people or areas?**  
No. Context scores summarize session conditions only. Community data should show observations, not unvalidated “danger scores.”

**How are hospitals and police mapped?**  
The repo includes a demo provider abstraction. Replace it with verified live place/provider data for production.

**Why will people use it outside an emergency?**  
Safety Readiness, Cyber Guardian, Privacy Guardian, nearby resources and routine journey check-ins create everyday utility.

**How will you satisfy 50-user validation?**  
Track unique users, meaningful sessions, completed journeys, cyber scans, readiness checks, privacy checks, feedback and testimonials.
