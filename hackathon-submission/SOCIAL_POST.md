## LinkedIn

Before I leave, what changed?

I built ShaktiSafe Brief as a reusable Community Rote Play: resolve an outing, check weather, modeled air quality and supported US official alerts, then compare a refresh with the saved baseline. It makes missing coverage and stale data visible instead of turning uncertainty into an “all clear.”

Tested with Houston, Chennai and London, with labeled synthetic failure demonstrations. Worldwide weather/air availability depends on providers; official alerts currently use NWS for supported US locations. Execution is subject to Rote process policy.
