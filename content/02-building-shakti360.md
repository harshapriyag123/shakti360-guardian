# Inside our 72-hour build

We focused on the engineering people rarely see: adaptive location policy, expiring sharing sessions, deterministic escalation, explainable scam signals, and resilient resource lookup.

The architecture separates bounded guardians from the Policy Engine. AI can organize or explain, but it cannot declare an emergency or contact police. That boundary shaped the API, interface, and tests.
