# Why women deserve proactive, privacy-first safety technology

Safety technology often asks women to choose between isolation and surveillance. Shakti360 begins from a different premise: preparation and connection should be temporary, consensual, and understandable.

A Safe Journey exists only for the trip. A guardian sees only what the user permits. A missed check-in follows a visible policy rather than an opaque prediction. Emergency services remain an explicit human choice.

This is not a claim that software can guarantee safety. It is a commitment to help people stay prepared, connected, and in control.
